About

I'm a Master's student in Computational Sciences at Laurentian University in Sudbury, working toward a career in Governance, Risk and Compliance and IT risk management.

What I enjoy most is the judgment part of GRC: working out how much harm a failure would really cause, picking controls that fit the system instead of ticking every box, and asking for proof rather than accepting a promise. I like writing short, defensible reasons that an auditor or an Authorizing Official can check quickly.

Right now I'm building hands-on projects around NIST RMF, FIPS 199, ISO 27001 and NIST CSF, and preparing for CompTIA Security+. I hold the Google Cybersecurity Certificate and have completed the TCS Cybersecurity Job Simulation.

Goal: GRC or IT risk analyst, helping organizations find, assess and reduce cybersecurity risk while keeping governance and compliance strong.
